Disease: Typhoid Fever
Category: Infectious Disease
Summary: A bacterial infection spread through contaminated food or water.
Symptoms:
- fever
- weakness
- headache
- abdominal pain
- loss of appetite
- constipation or diarrhea
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- severe abdominal pain
- blood in stool
- confusion
- dehydration
Causes:
- Salmonella Typhi bacteria
Risk Factors:
- unsafe water
- poor sanitation
- street food
Prevention:
- safe water
- handwashing
- food hygiene
- vaccination where recommended
Lifestyle Advice:
- hydration
- light diet
- complete prescribed antibiotics if given
Treatment Overview: Antibiotics are commonly required and should be prescribed by a doctor.
When To See A Doctor: If persistent fever, abdominal pain, or dehydration occurs.
