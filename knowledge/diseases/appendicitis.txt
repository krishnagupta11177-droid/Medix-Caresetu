Disease: Appendicitis
Category: Gastrointestinal
Summary: Inflammation of the appendix, often causing worsening abdominal pain.
Symptoms:
- pain near navel moving to right lower abdomen
- nausea
- vomiting
- fever
- loss of appetite
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- severe worsening pain
- rigid abdomen
- high fever
- inability to pass gas
Causes:
- blockage and infection of appendix
Risk Factors:
- young age
- family history
Prevention:
- no reliable prevention
Lifestyle Advice:
- seek early evaluation for severe abdominal pain
Treatment Overview: Appendicitis usually requires urgent surgical evaluation.
When To See A Doctor: Immediately if severe or worsening abdominal pain occurs.
