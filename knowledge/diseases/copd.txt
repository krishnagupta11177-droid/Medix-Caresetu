Disease: COPD
Category: Respiratory
Summary: A chronic lung disease causing airflow limitation, cough, and breathlessness.
Symptoms:
- chronic cough
- breathlessness
- wheezing
- chest tightness
- frequent chest infections
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- severe breathlessness
- confusion
- bluish lips
- worsening symptoms despite treatment
Causes:
- long-term exposure to irritants
- smoking
- biomass smoke
Risk Factors:
- smoking
- older age
- occupational dust or chemicals
Prevention:
- avoid smoking
- avoid smoke exposure
- vaccination where recommended
Lifestyle Advice:
- pulmonary rehabilitation
- breathing exercises
- avoid triggers
Treatment Overview: Long-term inhalers, lifestyle changes, and medical follow-up are commonly used.
When To See A Doctor: If breathing difficulty increases or infections become frequent.
