Disease: Peptic Ulcer Disease
Category: Gastrointestinal
Summary: Sores in the stomach or upper intestine, often associated with H. pylori or NSAID use.
Symptoms:
- burning stomach pain
- bloating
- nausea
- loss of appetite
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- vomiting blood
- black stool
- severe abdominal pain
- fainting
Causes:
- H. pylori infection
- NSAID use
Risk Factors:
- regular painkiller use
- smoking
- alcohol
Prevention:
- avoid unnecessary NSAIDs
- treat H. pylori where indicated
Lifestyle Advice:
- avoid smoking
- limit alcohol
- regular meals
Treatment Overview: Acid suppression and H. pylori treatment may be needed under medical care.
When To See A Doctor: Urgently if bleeding signs occur.
