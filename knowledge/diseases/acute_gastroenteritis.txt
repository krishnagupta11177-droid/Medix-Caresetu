Disease: Acute Gastroenteritis
Category: Gastrointestinal
Summary: An infection or inflammation of the stomach and intestines causing diarrhea and vomiting.
Symptoms:
- diarrhea
- vomiting
- abdominal cramps
- nausea
- fever
- weakness
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- bloody stool
- severe dehydration
- high fever
- severe abdominal pain
- persistent vomiting
Causes:
- viruses
- bacteria
- parasites
- contaminated food
Risk Factors:
- unsafe food
- unsafe water
- poor hand hygiene
Prevention:
- handwashing
- safe food
- clean water
Lifestyle Advice:
- ORS
- small frequent fluids
- light diet
Treatment Overview: Fluid replacement is key. Antibiotics are only needed for selected causes.
When To See A Doctor: If dehydration, blood in stool, or persistent vomiting occurs.
