Disease: Iron Deficiency Anemia
Category: Blood
Summary: A condition caused by insufficient iron, leading to low hemoglobin and fatigue.
Symptoms:
- fatigue
- pallor
- weakness
- shortness of breath
- dizziness
- brittle nails
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- chest pain
- severe breathlessness
- fainting
- rapid heartbeat
Causes:
- low iron intake
- blood loss
- heavy menstrual bleeding
- poor absorption
Risk Factors:
- menstruating women
- pregnancy
- vegetarian diet without adequate iron
- GI bleeding
Prevention:
- iron-rich diet
- treat heavy bleeding
- screening in high-risk groups
Lifestyle Advice:
- iron-rich foods
- vitamin C with iron sources
- avoid tea/coffee around meals if advised
Treatment Overview: Iron supplementation and investigation of the cause are commonly needed.
When To See A Doctor: If fatigue is severe, persistent, or associated with chest pain or fainting.
