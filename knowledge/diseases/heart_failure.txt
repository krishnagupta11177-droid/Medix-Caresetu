Disease: Heart Failure
Category: Cardiovascular
Summary: A condition where the heart cannot pump blood effectively enough for the body's needs.
Symptoms:
- breathlessness
- leg swelling
- fatigue
- reduced exercise tolerance
- night cough
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- severe breathlessness
- pink frothy sputum
- confusion
- chest pain
Causes:
- coronary disease
- hypertension
- cardiomyopathy
- valve disease
Risk Factors:
- older age
- diabetes
- hypertension
- previous heart attack
Prevention:
- control blood pressure
- diabetes care
- avoid smoking
Lifestyle Advice:
- salt restriction
- fluid monitoring where advised
- daily weight monitoring
Treatment Overview: Long-term medicines and monitoring are usually required.
When To See A Doctor: If swelling, breathlessness, or fatigue worsen.
