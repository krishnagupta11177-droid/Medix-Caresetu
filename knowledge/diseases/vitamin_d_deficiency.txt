Disease: Vitamin D Deficiency
Category: Nutritional
Summary: Low vitamin D levels can affect bones, muscles, and general well-being.
Symptoms:
- fatigue
- bone pain
- muscle weakness
- mood changes
- frequent infections
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- severe bone pain
- fractures with minimal injury
- severe muscle weakness
Causes:
- low sun exposure
- low dietary intake
- malabsorption
Risk Factors:
- indoor lifestyle
- older age
- dark skin
- obesity
Prevention:
- safe sun exposure
- fortified foods
- supplementation if advised
Lifestyle Advice:
- regular outdoor activity
- balanced diet
Treatment Overview: Supplementation is commonly used after testing.
When To See A Doctor: If fatigue, bone pain, or muscle weakness persist.
