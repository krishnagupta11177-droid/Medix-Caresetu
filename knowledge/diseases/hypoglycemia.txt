Disease: Hypoglycemia
Category: Endocrine
Summary: Abnormally low blood sugar, which can cause sweating, tremors, confusion, and weakness.
Symptoms:
- sweating
- tremors
- palpitations
- hunger
- weakness
- confusion
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- severe confusion
- seizure
- loss of consciousness
Causes:
- excess insulin or diabetes medicine
- skipped meals
- excess alcohol
- intense exercise
Risk Factors:
- diabetes treatment
- irregular meals
- kidney disease
Prevention:
- regular meals
- correct medicine dosing
- carry fast-acting carbohydrate if advised
Lifestyle Advice:
- monitor blood sugar
- do not skip meals
- recognize early symptoms
Treatment Overview: Fast-acting carbohydrate is commonly used for mild episodes. Severe cases need emergency care.
When To See A Doctor: If episodes are frequent, severe, or associated with confusion or fainting.
