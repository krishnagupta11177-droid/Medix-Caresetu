Disease: Common Cold
Category: Respiratory
Summary: A mild viral infection of the upper respiratory tract that usually improves on its own.
Symptoms:
- runny or blocked nose
- sore throat
- sneezing
- mild cough
- low-grade fever
- fatigue
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- difficulty breathing
- chest pain
- confusion
- dehydration
- fever lasting more than 3 days
- symptoms worsening after initial improvement
Causes:
- rhinoviruses and other respiratory viruses
Risk Factors:
- close contact with infected people
- poor hand hygiene
- weakened immune system
Prevention:
- wash hands regularly
- avoid touching face
- avoid close contact with sick people
- clean frequently touched surfaces
Lifestyle Advice:
- rest
- drink fluids
- warm saline gargles
- humidified air
Treatment Overview: Supportive care is usually sufficient. Antibiotics do not treat common cold viruses.
When To See A Doctor: If symptoms are severe, persistent, or red flags appear.
