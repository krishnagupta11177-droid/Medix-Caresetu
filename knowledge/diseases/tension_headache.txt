Disease: Tension-Type Headache
Category: Neurological
Summary: A common headache causing pressure or tightness, often related to stress or muscle tension.
Symptoms:
- pressure around head
- neck tension
- mild to moderate pain
- stress-related worsening
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- sudden severe headache
- weakness
- confusion
- fever
- vision changes
Causes:
- stress
- poor posture
- eye strain
- lack of sleep
Risk Factors:
- sedentary work
- anxiety
- screen strain
Prevention:
- stress management
- regular breaks
- good posture
- sleep hygiene
Lifestyle Advice:
- hydration
- stretching
- screen breaks
Treatment Overview: Lifestyle measures and occasional pain relief may help, but frequent headaches need evaluation.
When To See A Doctor: If headaches are frequent, worsening, or severe.
