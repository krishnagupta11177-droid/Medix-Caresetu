Disease: Chronic Kidney Disease
Category: Renal
Summary: A gradual loss of kidney function over time, often linked to diabetes or hypertension.
Symptoms:
- fatigue
- swelling
- reduced urine output
- nausea
- poor appetite
- itching
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- severe swelling
- breathlessness
- confusion
- very low urine output
Causes:
- diabetes
- hypertension
- glomerulonephritis
- polycystic kidney disease
Risk Factors:
- diabetes
- high blood pressure
- family history
- older age
Prevention:
- control diabetes and BP
- avoid unnecessary painkillers
- regular screening
Lifestyle Advice:
- salt control
- protein management if advised
- regular lab tests
Treatment Overview: Treatment focuses on slowing progression and managing complications.
When To See A Doctor: If kidney tests are abnormal or symptoms worsen.
