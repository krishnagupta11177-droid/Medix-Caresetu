Disease: Type 1 Diabetes
Category: Endocrine
Summary: An autoimmune condition where the body produces little or no insulin.
Symptoms:
- excessive thirst
- frequent urination
- weight loss
- fatigue
- increased hunger
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- vomiting
- confusion
- fruity breath
- severe weakness
- rapid breathing
Causes:
- autoimmune destruction of insulin-producing cells
Risk Factors:
- family history
- genetic predisposition
Prevention:
- no reliable prevention
- early diagnosis is important
Lifestyle Advice:
- insulin adherence
- carbohydrate awareness
- blood sugar monitoring
Treatment Overview: Insulin therapy is essential and must be supervised medically.
When To See A Doctor: Urgently if signs of diabetic ketoacidosis appear.
