Disease: Type 2 Diabetes
Category: Endocrine
Summary: A chronic condition where the body becomes resistant to insulin or does not use insulin effectively.
Symptoms:
- increased thirst
- frequent urination
- fatigue
- increased hunger
- slow wound healing
- blurred vision
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- severe dehydration
- confusion
- persistent vomiting
- very high blood sugar readings
Causes:
- insulin resistance
- genetic factors
- obesity
- sedentary lifestyle
Risk Factors:
- family history
- overweight
- physical inactivity
- gestational diabetes history
Prevention:
- healthy diet
- regular exercise
- weight management
- screening in high-risk groups
Lifestyle Advice:
- balanced meals
- portion control
- walking or exercise
- blood sugar monitoring
Treatment Overview: Lifestyle changes and medicines such as metformin or other agents may be prescribed by a doctor.
When To See A Doctor: If symptoms of high blood sugar occur or readings remain uncontrolled.
