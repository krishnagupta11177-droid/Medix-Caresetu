Disease: Coronary Artery Disease
Category: Cardiovascular
Summary: Narrowing of heart arteries, which can cause chest pain and heart attack.
Symptoms:
- chest discomfort
- shortness of breath
- fatigue
- pain in arm or jaw
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- severe chest pain
- sweating
- breathlessness
- fainting
- pain spreading to arm or jaw
Causes:
- atherosclerosis
Risk Factors:
- smoking
- diabetes
- hypertension
- high cholesterol
- family history
Prevention:
- control risk factors
- healthy diet
- exercise
- avoid smoking
Lifestyle Advice:
- cardiac rehabilitation where advised
- regular follow-up
Treatment Overview: Medicines, lifestyle changes, and procedures may be required under specialist care.
When To See A Doctor: Urgently if chest pain or heart attack symptoms occur.
