Disease: Tuberculosis
Category: Infectious Disease
Summary: A bacterial infection that most commonly affects the lungs and can be serious if untreated.
Symptoms:
- cough lasting more than 2 weeks
- weight loss
- night sweats
- fever
- fatigue
- blood in sputum
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- coughing blood
- severe weight loss
- difficulty breathing
- persistent fever
Causes:
- Mycobacterium tuberculosis
Risk Factors:
- close contact with active TB
- weak immune system
- crowded living conditions
Prevention:
- early diagnosis
- treatment of active cases
- ventilation
- contact screening
Lifestyle Advice:
- complete prescribed treatment
- nutrition support
- avoid close contact until advised
Treatment Overview: TB requires a full course of prescribed antibiotics and medical supervision.
When To See A Doctor: If persistent cough, fever, night sweats, or weight loss occurs.
