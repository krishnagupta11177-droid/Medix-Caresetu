Disease: Urinary Tract Infection
Category: Renal/Urological
Summary: An infection of the urinary tract, commonly causing burning urination and frequency.
Symptoms:
- burning urination
- frequent urination
- urgent urination
- lower abdominal discomfort
- cloudy urine
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- fever
- back pain
- vomiting
- blood in urine
- confusion in older adults
Causes:
- bacterial infection
Risk Factors:
- female sex
- dehydration
- holding urine
- catheter use
Prevention:
- hydration
- good hygiene
- avoid holding urine
Lifestyle Advice:
- fluid intake
- complete prescribed treatment
Treatment Overview: Antibiotics are commonly required and should be prescribed after evaluation.
When To See A Doctor: If fever, back pain, or symptoms persist.
