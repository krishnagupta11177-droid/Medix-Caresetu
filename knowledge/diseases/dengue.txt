Disease: Dengue
Category: Infectious Disease
Summary: A mosquito-borne viral illness that can cause high fever, rash, and body pain.
Symptoms:
- high fever
- severe headache
- pain behind eyes
- muscle and joint pain
- rash
- nausea
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- severe abdominal pain
- persistent vomiting
- bleeding gums
- blood in vomit or stool
- restlessness
Causes:
- dengue virus transmitted by Aedes mosquitoes
Risk Factors:
- mosquito exposure
- stagnant water
- previous dengue infection
Prevention:
- eliminate stagnant water
- mosquito repellents
- screens
- protective clothing
Lifestyle Advice:
- hydration
- rest
- platelet and warning-sign monitoring where advised
Treatment Overview: Supportive care and hydration are important. Severe dengue needs urgent hospital care.
When To See A Doctor: Immediately if warning signs or severe symptoms occur.
