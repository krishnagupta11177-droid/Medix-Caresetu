Disease: Hepatitis B
Category: Liver
Summary: A viral liver infection spread through blood or body fluids.
Symptoms:
- fatigue
- jaundice
- dark urine
- abdominal discomfort
- loss of appetite
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- severe jaundice
- confusion
- swelling
- bleeding tendency
Causes:
- hepatitis B virus
Risk Factors:
- unprotected sex
- needle exposure
- mother-to-child transmission
Prevention:
- vaccination
- safe injection practices
- screening
Lifestyle Advice:
- regular liver monitoring
- avoid alcohol
Treatment Overview: Some cases require antiviral treatment and long-term monitoring.
When To See A Doctor: If jaundice or liver symptoms occur.
