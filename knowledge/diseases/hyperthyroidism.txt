Disease: Hyperthyroidism
Category: Endocrine
Summary: A condition where the thyroid gland produces too much thyroid hormone.
Symptoms:
- weight loss
- rapid heartbeat
- anxiety
- heat intolerance
- tremor
- loose stools
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- severe palpitations
- chest pain
- difficulty breathing
- severe agitation
Causes:
- Graves disease
- thyroid nodules
- thyroiditis
Risk Factors:
- family history
- autoimmune disease
- iodine excess
Prevention:
- regular screening in high-risk groups
- avoid excess iodine unless advised
Lifestyle Advice:
- regular follow-up
- manage palpitations and stress
Treatment Overview: Antithyroid medicines, beta blockers, or other treatments may be prescribed.
When To See A Doctor: If symptoms of overactive thyroid or heart symptoms occur.
