Disease: Vitamin B12 Deficiency
Category: Nutritional
Summary: A deficiency that can cause anemia, fatigue, and nerve-related symptoms.
Symptoms:
- fatigue
- weakness
- numbness or tingling
- poor balance
- pale skin
- mouth soreness
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- severe weakness
- difficulty walking
- confusion
Causes:
- low dietary intake
- malabsorption
- certain medicines
- gastric surgery
Risk Factors:
- vegetarian or vegan diet
- older age
- intestinal disease
Prevention:
- adequate dietary intake
- supplementation when needed
Lifestyle Advice:
- B12-rich foods or fortified foods
- regular testing if high risk
Treatment Overview: Supplementation is often required, especially in symptomatic deficiency.
When To See A Doctor: If fatigue, tingling, balance problems, or anemia symptoms occur.
