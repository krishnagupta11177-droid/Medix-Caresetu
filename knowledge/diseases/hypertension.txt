Disease: Hypertension
Category: Cardiovascular
Summary: Persistently high blood pressure, which increases risk of heart disease, stroke, and kidney disease.
Symptoms:
- often no symptoms
- headache
- dizziness
- nosebleeds in severe cases
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- severe headache
- chest pain
- difficulty breathing
- vision changes
- confusion
- weakness
Causes:
- genetics
- excess salt
- obesity
- stress
- kidney disease
Risk Factors:
- family history
- high salt diet
- sedentary lifestyle
- obesity
- alcohol use
Prevention:
- reduce salt
- exercise regularly
- maintain healthy weight
- limit alcohol
- manage stress
Lifestyle Advice:
- home BP monitoring
- DASH-style diet
- regular activity
- sleep hygiene
Treatment Overview: Lifestyle modification and prescribed antihypertensive medicines are commonly used.
When To See A Doctor: If readings are repeatedly high or red-flag symptoms occur.
