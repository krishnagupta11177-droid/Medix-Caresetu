Disease: Dehydration
Category: General
Summary: A condition caused by excessive loss or insufficient intake of fluids.
Symptoms:
- thirst
- dry mouth
- fatigue
- dizziness
- dark urine
- reduced urination
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- confusion
- very low urine output
- weak pulse
- fainting
- severe lethargy
Causes:
- diarrhea
- vomiting
- fever
- excess sweating
- poor fluid intake
Risk Factors:
- children
- older adults
- illness with fluid loss
Prevention:
- regular fluids
- ORS during illness
- avoid excessive heat exposure
Lifestyle Advice:
- sip fluids regularly
- monitor urine color
Treatment Overview: Mild dehydration can often be treated with fluids. Severe dehydration requires urgent care.
When To See A Doctor: If severe weakness, confusion, or very low urine output occurs.
