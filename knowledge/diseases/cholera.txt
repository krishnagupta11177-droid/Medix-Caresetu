Disease: Cholera
Category: Infectious Disease
Summary: A bacterial infection causing severe watery diarrhea and dehydration.
Symptoms:
- watery diarrhea
- vomiting
- weakness
- muscle cramps
- dehydration
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- severe dehydration
- sunken eyes
- very low urine output
- confusion
- weak pulse
Causes:
- Vibrio cholerae from contaminated water or food
Risk Factors:
- unsafe drinking water
- poor sanitation
Prevention:
- safe water
- oral rehydration
- hand hygiene
- sanitation
Lifestyle Advice:
- ORS and fluids
- early medical care
Treatment Overview: Rehydration is essential. Antibiotics may be used in severe cases under medical guidance.
When To See A Doctor: Urgently if severe diarrhea or dehydration occurs.
