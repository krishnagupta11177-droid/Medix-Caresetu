Disease: Kidney Stones
Category: Renal
Summary: Hard deposits in the kidney or urinary tract that can cause severe pain.
Symptoms:
- severe flank pain
- pain radiating to groin
- blood in urine
- nausea
- painful urination
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- fever with pain
- uncontrolled vomiting
- difficulty passing urine
- severe pain
Causes:
- concentrated urine
- dietary factors
- metabolic causes
Risk Factors:
- low fluid intake
- high salt diet
- family history
Prevention:
- adequate hydration
- dietary modifications based on stone type
Lifestyle Advice:
- drink fluids regularly
- medical evaluation of recurrent stones
Treatment Overview: Pain control and hydration are common. Larger stones may need procedures.
When To See A Doctor: If pain is severe, fever occurs, or urine output decreases.
