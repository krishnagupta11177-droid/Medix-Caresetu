Disease: Hypothyroidism
Category: Endocrine
Summary: A condition where the thyroid gland does not produce enough thyroid hormone.
Symptoms:
- fatigue
- weight gain
- cold intolerance
- constipation
- dry skin
- hair loss
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- severe swelling
- confusion
- very slow heart rate
- difficulty breathing
Causes:
- Hashimoto thyroiditis
- iodine deficiency
- post-treatment hypothyroidism
Risk Factors:
- female sex
- autoimmune disease
- family history
Prevention:
- screening in high-risk groups
- appropriate iodine intake
Lifestyle Advice:
- take prescribed thyroid medicine consistently
- regular testing
Treatment Overview: Thyroid hormone replacement is commonly prescribed and monitored.
When To See A Doctor: If fatigue, weight gain, constipation, or cold intolerance persist.
