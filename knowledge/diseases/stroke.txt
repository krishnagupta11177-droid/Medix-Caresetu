Disease: Stroke
Category: Neurological
Summary: A medical emergency caused by interrupted blood flow to the brain or bleeding in the brain.
Symptoms:
- face drooping
- arm weakness
- speech difficulty
- sudden numbness
- sudden confusion
- sudden vision trouble
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- any sudden stroke symptom
- severe sudden headache
- loss of balance
- difficulty speaking
Causes:
- blocked artery
- bleeding in brain
Risk Factors:
- hypertension
- diabetes
- smoking
- heart disease
- high cholesterol
Prevention:
- control blood pressure
- diabetes control
- avoid smoking
- healthy diet
Lifestyle Advice:
- regular exercise
- medical adherence
- stroke awareness
Treatment Overview: Emergency treatment is time-critical. Immediate hospital care is required.
When To See A Doctor: Call emergency services immediately if stroke symptoms appear.
