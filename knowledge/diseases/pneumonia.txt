Disease: Pneumonia
Category: Respiratory
Summary: An infection of the lungs that can cause cough, fever, and breathing difficulty.
Symptoms:
- cough
- fever
- chest pain while breathing or coughing
- fatigue
- shortness of breath
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- severe breathing difficulty
- bluish lips
- confusion
- persistent high fever
- dehydration
Causes:
- bacteria
- viruses
- fungi
Risk Factors:
- older age
- chronic lung disease
- smoking
- weak immune system
Prevention:
- vaccination where recommended
- hand hygiene
- avoid smoking
- respiratory hygiene
Lifestyle Advice:
- rest
- fluids
- avoid smoking
- follow-up care
Treatment Overview: Treatment depends on cause. Bacterial pneumonia may require antibiotics prescribed by a doctor.
When To See A Doctor: Immediately if breathing difficulty, chest pain, confusion, or low oxygen occurs.
