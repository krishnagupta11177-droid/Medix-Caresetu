Disease: GERD
Category: Gastrointestinal
Summary: A condition where stomach acid frequently flows back into the food pipe.
Symptoms:
- heartburn
- acid taste
- chest discomfort
- regurgitation
- chronic cough
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- difficulty swallowing
- vomiting blood
- black stool
- unexplained weight loss
Causes:
- lower esophageal sphincter dysfunction
Risk Factors:
- obesity
- late-night meals
- smoking
- certain foods
Prevention:
- avoid large late meals
- reduce trigger foods
- maintain healthy weight
Lifestyle Advice:
- elevate head while sleeping
- avoid lying down after meals
Treatment Overview: Lifestyle changes and acid-reducing medicines are commonly used.
When To See A Doctor: If symptoms are persistent or red flags occur.
