Disease: Migraine
Category: Neurological
Summary: A recurrent headache disorder often associated with throbbing pain, nausea, and light sensitivity.
Symptoms:
- throbbing headache
- nausea
- vomiting
- sensitivity to light or sound
- visual aura
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- sudden severe headache
- weakness
- confusion
- vision loss
- fever with stiff neck
Causes:
- genetic and environmental factors
Risk Factors:
- stress
- sleep disturbance
- hormonal changes
- certain foods
Prevention:
- trigger identification
- regular sleep
- hydration
- stress management
Lifestyle Advice:
- dark quiet room during attacks
- regular meals
- sleep hygiene
Treatment Overview: Acute and preventive medicines may be prescribed depending on frequency.
When To See A Doctor: If headaches are severe, frequent, or associated with neurological symptoms.
