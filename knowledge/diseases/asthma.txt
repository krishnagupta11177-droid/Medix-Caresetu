Disease: Asthma
Category: Respiratory
Summary: A chronic airway condition causing episodes of wheezing, breathlessness, chest tightness, and cough.
Symptoms:
- wheezing
- shortness of breath
- chest tightness
- cough
- nighttime symptoms
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- severe breathlessness
- difficulty speaking full sentences
- reliever inhaler not helping
- bluish lips
Causes:
- airway inflammation and hyperresponsiveness
Risk Factors:
- allergies
- family history
- smoke exposure
- air pollution
- dust mites
Prevention:
- avoid triggers
- use controller medicine if prescribed
- avoid smoke
- vaccination where advised
Lifestyle Advice:
- trigger control
- carry reliever inhaler if prescribed
- regular review
Treatment Overview: Controller and reliever inhalers are commonly used under medical guidance.
When To See A Doctor: If symptoms are frequent, worsening, or reliever is needed often.
