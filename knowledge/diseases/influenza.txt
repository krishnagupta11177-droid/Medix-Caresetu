Disease: Influenza
Category: Respiratory
Summary: A contagious respiratory viral illness that often causes fever, body aches, and fatigue.
Symptoms:
- fever
- chills
- body aches
- headache
- dry cough
- sore throat
- fatigue
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- difficulty breathing
- persistent chest pain
- confusion
- severe weakness
- bluish lips
- dehydration
Causes:
- influenza A or B viruses
Risk Factors:
- older age
- young children
- pregnancy
- chronic disease
- weak immunity
Prevention:
- annual flu vaccination
- hand hygiene
- avoiding sick contacts
- covering coughs and sneezes
Lifestyle Advice:
- rest
- fluids
- fever monitoring
- isolation while symptomatic
Treatment Overview: Rest, fluids, fever control, and medical assessment for high-risk patients.
When To See A Doctor: If breathing difficulty, confusion, persistent fever, or dehydration occurs.
