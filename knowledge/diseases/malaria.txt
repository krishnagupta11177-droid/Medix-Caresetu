Disease: Malaria
Category: Infectious Disease
Summary: A mosquito-borne parasitic illness causing fever, chills, and fatigue.
Symptoms:
- fever
- chills
- sweating
- headache
- body aches
- fatigue
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- confusion
- severe weakness
- difficulty breathing
- persistent vomiting
- dark urine
Causes:
- Plasmodium parasites transmitted by Anopheles mosquitoes
Risk Factors:
- travel to endemic areas
- lack of mosquito protection
Prevention:
- mosquito nets
- repellents
- indoor spraying
- preventive treatment where advised
Lifestyle Advice:
- early testing
- complete prescribed treatment
Treatment Overview: Antimalarial treatment should be guided by testing and medical care.
When To See A Doctor: Urgently if fever occurs after travel to malaria areas.
