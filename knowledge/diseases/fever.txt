Disease: Fever
Category: General
Summary: An elevated body temperature, often caused by infection or inflammation.
Symptoms:
- warm body
- chills
- sweating
- weakness
- headache
- loss of appetite
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- very high fever
- confusion
- difficulty breathing
- severe headache
- rash
- stiff neck
- dehydration
Causes:
- infections
- inflammatory conditions
- heat illness
- some medicines
Risk Factors:
- infection exposure
- weak immune system
- chronic illness
Prevention:
- hand hygiene
- safe food and water
- vaccination where appropriate
Lifestyle Advice:
- rest
- fluids
- light clothing
- temperature monitoring
Treatment Overview: Treat discomfort and monitor warning signs. Persistent fever needs medical evaluation.
When To See A Doctor: If fever is high, persistent, or associated with red flags.
