Disease: Hepatitis A
Category: Liver
Summary: A viral liver infection spread mainly through contaminated food or water.
Symptoms:
- fatigue
- nausea
- abdominal pain
- dark urine
- jaundice
Red Flags / Emergency Warning Signs:
- severe abdominal pain
- confusion
- persistent vomiting
- severe jaundice
Causes:
- hepatitis A virus
Risk Factors:
- unsafe water
- poor sanitation
Prevention:
- handwashing
- safe water
- vaccination where recommended
Lifestyle Advice:
- rest
- hydration
- avoid alcohol
Treatment Overview: Usually supportive care; severe cases need hospital assessment.
When To See A Doctor: If jaundice, severe vomiting, or confusion occurs.
